1. MOLE CONCEPT - 30 Marks in UNEB

A) Definitions

  • Relative Atomic Mass (Ar): mass compared to 1/12 Carbon-12
  • Relative Molecular Mass (Mr): sum of Ar
  • Mole: 6.02x10^23 particles
Mr H2O = 2(1)+16=18
Mr H2SO4=2+32+64=98
Mr CaCO3=40+12+48=100

B) Calculations

moles = mass / Mr
moles = volume(dm3)/22.4 at STP
Molarity = moles / litres
Conc (g/dm3) = Molarity x Mr

Example 1: 11g CO2 is how many moles? Mr CO2=44. moles=11/44=0.25

Example 2: What volume does 0.5 moles O2 occupy at STP? 0.5x22.4=11.2 dm3

C) Empirical & Molecular

Steps: % → mass (100g) → moles → ratio → simplest

Ex: Compound 40% C, 6.7% H, 53.3% O. C:40/12=3.33, H:6.7/1=6.7, O:53.3/16=3.33. Ratio 1:2:1 → CH2O. If Mr=180, (CH2O)n=180 →30n=180→n=6→ C6H12O6

D) Stoichiometry

CaCO3 + 2HCl → CaCl2 + H2O + CO2. If 10g CaCO3 used, what mass HCl needed? Moles CaCO3=10/100=0.1, HCl moles=0.2, mass=0.2x36.5=7.3g

2. ELECTROLYSIS - Practical comes

  • Electrolyte: conducts & decomposes
  • Cathode (-) : cations go, Reduction, metal/H2 produced
  • Anode (+) : anions go, Oxidation, non-metal/O2 produced

Key Electrolyses

  • Brine (NaCl aq): Cathode H2, Anode Cl2, NaOH left
  • Water (acidified): Cathode 2H2, Anode O2, ratio 2:1
  • CuSO4 with Cu electrodes: Cathode Cu increases, Anode Cu decreases - purification
  • CuSO4 with Carbon: Cathode Cu, Anode O2

Factors

Concentration, Position in reactivity series, Nature of electrode

3. RATES & EQUILIBRIUM

Rate

  • Measure: amount/time
  • Speed up: Temp↑, Conc↑, Size small (powder), Catalyst, Pressure (gases)
  • Collision theory: more collisions = faster

Equilibrium

Reversible reaction: N2+3H2 ⇌ 2NH3

Le Chatelier: If temp ↑, equilibrium shifts to absorb heat. If pressure ↑, shifts to side with less moles.

Haber process: N2+3H2 high pressure 200atm, 450C, Fe catalyst → NH3 for fertilizer

4. ORGANIC CHEMISTRY S4

Alkanes CnH2n+2

  • Single bonds, saturated, unreactive except combustion
  • Methane CH4 +2O2→CO2+2H2O
  • Substitution: CH4+Cl2→CH3Cl+HCl (UV light)

Alkenes CnH2n

  • Double bond, unsaturated, reactive
  • Test: Decolorizes bromine water (orange→colourless)
  • Addition: C2H4+H2→C2H6, C2H4+Br2→C2H4Br2
  • Polymerization: nC2H4→ (C2H4)n polythene

Alcohols -OH

  • Ethanol from fermentation: C6H12O6 →2C2H5OH+2CO2 (yeast, 30C)
  • Properties: Burns, Oxidized to ethanoic acid, Dehydrated to ethene (conc H2SO4)

Acids -COOH

  • Ethanoic CH3COOH + NaOH→CH3COONa+H2O
  • Esterification: CH3COOH+C2H5OH ⇌ CH3COOC2H5+H2O (conc H2SO4)

5. EXTRACTION & ENVIRONMENT

  • Fe: Blast furnace 1500C, Coke C+O2→CO2, CO2+C→2CO, Fe2O3+3CO→2Fe+3CO2, Limestone removes impurity CaCO3→CaO+CO2, CaO+SiO2→CaSiO3 slag
  • Al: Electrolysis of Al2O3 in cryolite
  • Pollution: CO from cars, SO2 acid rain H2O+SO2→H2SO3 damages buildings, CO2 global warming
