📘 S1: Matter & Separation

States: Solid fixed, Liquid no fixed shape, Gas no fixed shape/volume. Sublimation Iodine/NH4Cl. Separation: Filtration, Evaporation, Distillation, Chromatography.

Elements, Compounds, Mixtures: Element pure, Compound chemically combined, Mixture physically.

📗 S2: Atom & Bonding

Atom: Protons+ neutrons nucleus, electrons shells. Atomic No=protons, Mass No=protons+neutrons. Isotopes Cl-35/37. Config Na 2.8.1 Cl 2.8.7

Periodic: Groups vertical same valence, Periods horizontal same shells. Gp I alkali reactivity increases down.

Bonding: Ionic transfer metal+non-metal NaCl high mp. Covalent share H2O. Formula Al2O3 swap valency.

📙 S3: Acids Bases Carbon

Acid: H+ pH<7 blue→red HCl H2SO4. Base OH- pH>7 red→blue NaOH. Acid+Base=Salt+Water. Acid+Metal=Salt+H2. Acid+Carbonate=Salt+Water+CO2

Hard water: Ca2+/Mg2+. Temporary boiling, permanent Na2CO3. Carbon: Diamond hard, Graphite soft conducts. CO2 test lime water milky.

📕 S4: MOLE (30 Marks UNEB)

Moles=Mass/Mr. Molarity=Moles/Litres. 1 mole gas STP=22.4 dm3. Avogadro 6.02x10^23
Ex: 11g CO2=0.25 moles (Mr44). %Fe in Fe2O3=70%

Electrolysis: Cathode- Reduction metal/H2, Anode+ Oxidation Cl2/O2. Brine→Cl2+H2+NaOH. Water→H2+O2 2:1.

Rates: ↑ temp, conc, small particles, catalyst, pressure. Collision theory.

Organic: Alkanes CnH2n+2 single saturated CH4. Alkenes CnH2n double unsaturated decolorizes Br2 water polythene. Ethanol fermentation C6H12O6→2C2H5OH+2CO2. Ester acid+alcohol.

Extraction: K>Na>Ca>Mg>Al>Zn>Fe>Pb>H>Cu>Ag>Au. Fe2O3+3CO→2Fe+3CO2 blast furnace.
