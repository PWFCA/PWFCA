📘 S1: INTRODUCTION & MATTER

▼

1. What is Chemistry?

Study of composition, properties, changes of matter.

2. Lab Safety

  • Apparatus: Beaker, Flask, Test tube, Bunsen, Pipette, Burette
  • Rules: No tasting, No eating, Wear goggles
First Aid: Acid on skin → wash water → NaHCO3. Alkali → wash → vinegar.

3. States of Matter

  • Solid: fixed shape/volume
  • Liquid: no fixed shape
  • Gas: no fixed shape/volume
  • Changes: Melting, Evaporation, Sublimation (Iodine, NH4Cl)

4. Elements, Compounds, Mixtures & Separation

  • Element: cannot be split (Na, Fe)
  • Compound: chemically combined (H2O)
  • Mixture: physically combined (sand+water)
  • Methods: Filtration, Evaporation, Distillation, Chromatography, Magnetic

📗 S2: ATOMIC STRUCTURE & BONDING

▼

Atomic Structure

Protons (+), Neutrons in nucleus, Electrons in shells.

Atomic No = protons
Mass No = protons+neutrons
Isotopes: same protons diff neutrons e.g Cl-35/37

Config: Na 2.8.1, Cl 2.8.7

Periodic Table

  • Groups vertical = same valence. Group I Alkali, VII Halogen, 0 Noble
  • Periods horizontal = same shells
  • Group I reactivity increases down

Bonding

  • Ionic: transfer, metal+non-metal, NaCl, high m.p, conducts molten
  • Covalent: share, non-metal+non-metal, H2O, low m.p
  • Metallic: free electrons
Valency swap: Al 3 + O 2 = Al2O3

📙 S3: ACIDS, BASES, WATER & CARBON

▼

Acids, Bases, Salts

  • Acid: H+ in water, pH<7, blue→red, HCl, H2SO4
  • Base: OH-, pH>7, red→blue, NaOH
  • Acid+Base=Salt+Water
  • Acid+Metal=Salt+H2 e.g Zn+2HCl→ZnCl2+H2
  • Acid+Carbonate=Salt+Water+CO2

Water & Hardness

  • Hard water has Ca2+/Mg2+
  • Temporary Ca(HCO3)2 removed by boiling
  • Permanent CaSO4 removed by Na2CO3

Carbon

  • Diamond hard tetrahedron
