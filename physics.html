1. Measurements

Fundamental quantities: Length (m), Mass (kg), Time (s), Temperature (K)

Density = Mass / Volume

Unit: kg/m³ or g/cm³

Example: Mass 100g, Volume 50cm³
Density = 100/50 = 2 g/cm³

2. Motion - Speed & Velocity

Speed = Distance / Time (m/s)

Velocity = Displacement / Time

Acceleration = (v - u) / t

Equations: v = u + at, s = ut + ½at²

3. Forces

Force = Mass × Acceleration (F=ma)

Unit: Newton (N)

Weight = Mass × gravity (W=mg)

4. UNEB Past Question

Q: Define density and state its SI unit.

A: Density is mass per unit volume. SI unit is kg/m³.

Q: A stone mass 120g has volume 40cm³. Find density.

A: 120/40 = 3 g/cm³
